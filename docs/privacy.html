Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Thiện AI Thực Chiến ("we", "our", or "the Application") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when connecting to TikTok APIs.

1. Information We Access

With your OAuth authorization, the Application accesses basic profile information (user.info.basic, user.info.stats), video publishing permissions (video.upload, video.publish), and published video performance metrics (video.list).

2. How We Use Information

Data is used exclusively to upload educational short videos to the authorized TikTok account and analyze view/engagement metrics to improve video topics. We do not sell, rent, or share any user data with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Revocation

OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in GitHub Actions Secrets. Users may revoke access at any time from their TikTok account settings.
